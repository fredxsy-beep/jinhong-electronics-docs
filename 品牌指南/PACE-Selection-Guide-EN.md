## Product Lines Overview

The PACE range falls into five blocks. First, **single-channel soldering stations**, represented by the ADS200 PLUS, built on the AccuDrive temperature-control platform and using integrated tips. Second, **multi-channel soldering/rework stations**: the MBT 360 / MBT 450 integrate a soldering iron, thermal tweezers and a desoldering tool in a single unit, and handpieces for the AccuDrive and SensaTemp heating platforms are interchangeable between them. Third, **rework workstations and hot-air rework**: the ST 125 has a built-in vacuum pump covering both hot air and pick-up, and the ST 925 is a programmable hot-air rework system. Fourth, **area-array rework**: the TF series (convection heating) and IR series (infrared heating) are dedicated to BGA/SMD rework on large boards. Fifth, **fume extraction** (Arm series extraction arms and filtration systems), configured together with the rework station. Models currently supplied through Jinhong include the ADS200, ST 125, ST 115E, MBT 360, MBT 350, ST 925, IR 1000 and Arm-150 fume extraction; stations can be completed for an existing bench or built new as a whole station.

## Selection Guide

1. **Distinguish "mainly soldering" from "mainly rework" first.** For benches centred on soldering and touch-up, choose the ADS200 PLUS or ST 25. As soon as desoldering, solder suction or hot-air component removal is involved, go directly to a rework platform with a vacuum pump (ST 125 or the MBT series) — adding desoldering tools later to a single soldering station costs more.
2. **Choose a multi-channel station where several tools share one bench.** At rework benches where a soldering iron, thermal tweezers and a desoldering tool are used in rotation, one MBT 360 main unit sharing three ports saves bench space and sockets compared with three separate units, and automatic shut-off in the tool stands also extends tip life; choose the MBT 450 where display and process monitoring are required.
3. **For BGA rework, choose between convection and infrared by board size and heating method.** The convection TF series offers vision alignment and active cooling, suited to high-thermal-mass boards and precise component replacement; the infrared IR series needs no nozzle changes by component and gives a uniform temperature field, suited to high-mix, small-batch work. For boards up to 12″ × 12″ choose the TF 1800 / IR 3100; for larger boards choose the TF 2800 / IR 4100.
4. **Match tips by family, not by shape alone.** AccuDrive Blue Series and SensaTemp are two separate families, and handpieces and tips must be matched within the same family; integrated tips build the sensor and heating element into the tip, so any single tip can be set to any temperature and there is no need to stock tips by temperature grade.
5. **Plan fume extraction together with the rework bench.** Rework benches generate a large amount of solder fume; Arm series extraction arms and filtration systems should be configured when the bench is built, as later retrofitting is often limited by bench space and ducting.

## FAQ

**Q: How do I choose between the ADS200 PLUS and the MBT 360?**
A: The ADS200 PLUS is a single-channel soldering station, suited to benches centred on soldering. The MBT 360 is a three-port multi-channel station with a soldering iron, thermal tweezers and a desoldering tool online at the same time, suited to benches with frequent desoldering and rework tasks. If the task involves solder suction and tweezer rework at all, go directly to the MBT series.

**Q: For BGA rework, should I choose convection (TF) or infrared (IR)?**
A: The convection type heats through a hot-air nozzle and offers vision alignment and placement functions, suited to cases where a component must be removed and placed back and the board has a large thermal mass; the infrared type uses no nozzle and non-contact closed-loop temperature measurement, so no nozzle change is needed when changing product types. Then match the power class by board size: small boards TF 1800 / IR 3100, large boards TF 2800 / IR 4100.

**Q: Why are tips divided into two families, and can they be mixed?**
A: No. AccuDrive is the new-generation integrated-tip platform and SensaTemp is the established platform; their handpiece interfaces and temperature-control methods differ. When purchasing, confirm the family of the handpiece in use first, then stock tips within that family to avoid buying the wrong type.
