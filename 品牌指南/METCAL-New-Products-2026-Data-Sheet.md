## CV-IoT — Intelligent IoT Soldering System

The CV-IoT makes real-time process control and traceability easy. It records all soldering data in real time — soldering time, temperature, power, result, and the cartridge used — and provides access to dynamic or static reports that can be generated locally or anywhere in the world, connecting directly to your network. The intuitive dashboard shows soldering events, cartridge life, shop-floor updates (highlighting how many stations are on/off), tip fault events and the total solder joint count per station. Data is generated in real time for at-a-glance productivity and system status; views can be optimized with filters and user configurations; detailed data can be downloaded to CSV files for evaluation and queries; and a barcode scanner (not included) can be added to scan printed circuit boards or other items.

Supplied with the system: CVI-IOT gateway; charger, 5 V, 500 mA; software instruction guide; USB 2.0 A male-to-male cable; USB 2.0 A splitter cable; USB 2.0 A to Micro USB male cable. System requirements: Windows 10; Intel® Core i5 CPU or higher; 2 GB system memory/RAM; Intel® HD graphics; at least 150 GB of storage space; Ethernet 10/100.

## Selection Notes

1. **Adjustable temperature vs SmartHeat.** Choose the GT120 where a settable temperature range (150 - 450 °C) and tip presets are required in one station; choose SmartHeat systems (MX / CV / MFR) where the temperature grade is fixed by the cartridge for maximum repeatability.
2. **Micro work.** For joints down to 01005 components and 0.1 mm geometries under the microscope, add MicroFine handpieces and MFTC / MFT cartridges to existing MX or CV systems instead of buying a separate station.
3. **Through-hole desoldering.** The MFR-1350's internal 0.7 bar vacuum pump and switchable dual ports suit mixed soldering/desoldering benches; pick DxP tips by hole diameter (DCP-CN3 / CN5 / CN7 are the Flex-recommended sizes).
4. **Hot-air rework.** The HCT-910 (900 W, 50 - 600 °C) covers demanding SMD rework; pair it with a PT4 preheater and BH board holder for large, thick or multilayer boards so the assembly is preheated uniformly before rework.
5. **Fume extraction.** One bench: BVX-100 or an MSA absorber (MSA-25U where USB power and GT-station sleep linkage are useful; MSA-35L for dual-position use). Two stations or higher airflow: BVX-250, which also reports filter status to CV and GT systems.
6. **Traceability.** Where every joint must be recorded — time, temperature, power, result and cartridge — specify CV-IoT on top of the CV platform.

## FAQ

**Q: Does the GT120 use the same cartridges across thermal demands?**
A: The T6 handpiece accepts both split and one-piece cartridges and serves low and high thermal demand applications with a single handpiece; cartridges are sold separately (the GT120 system is GT120-HP-T6).

**Q: Can MicroFine be added to a station we already own?**
A: Yes. MicroFine handpieces and tweezers are compatible with all MX and CV soldering systems; no stand-alone unit is required. Order the UK kits (e.g. MX-UK9, CV-UK10) if a workstand is also needed.

**Q: What is included with the CV-IoT system?**
A: The CVI-IOT gateway, a 5 V / 500 mA charger, the software instruction guide and the USB cable set listed above. A barcode scanner is not included. Host PC requirements are Windows 10 with a Core i5 or higher CPU, 2 GB RAM and at least 150 GB of storage.

**Q: Which preheater fits a 300 mm wide board?**
A: The PT4-8000 (total heating area 360 x 400 mm, with the BH-8000 holder accepting PCBs up to 378 x 418 mm). The PT4-4000 covers boards up to its 240 x 240 mm heating area (BH-4000 holder up to 258 x 258 mm).
