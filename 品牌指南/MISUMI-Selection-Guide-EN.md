## Product Lines Overview

This guide covers four product lines regularly supplied by Jinhong. First, **fasteners** (CB hex socket head bolts), the high-strength standard parts used in the largest quantities in die, mould and equipment assembly. Second, **locating parts** (MS straight dowel pins), the datum parts for die and mould closing and fixture positioning. Third, **linear motion parts** (SSEBZ miniature linear guides and C-TBSB12 economy rolled ball screws), used for equipment feed and positioning transmission. Fourth, **die and mould parts** (RBSH ball bearing guide post sets for die sets, SWF rectangular-section coil springs, and SP shoulder punches), the core standard parts for stamping die guiding, stripping and punching. The common logic of MISUMI selection is "specifications as values": once the key values — diameter, length, tolerance, strength grade and so on — are fully stated, the model number is determined. Giving only a product name (such as "hex socket bolt" or "dowel pin") is the main source of wrong model numbers and repeated confirmations.

## Selection Guide

1. **State all values when asking for a quotation.** For fasteners, state thread specification + length; for dowel pins, diameter + length + tolerance; for springs, outside diameter + free length + required load; for ball screws, stroke + load + mounting method. A product name alone cannot fix a model number, and is also the main cause of repeated delivery-date confirmations. If you have an existing part in use, a photo of the actual part or the original model number helps checking.
2. **Select strength and hardness by grade.** CB bolts are grade 12.9 for M3–M20 and grade 10.9 for M24; for load-critical positions prefer grade 12.9 and tighten to the specified torque. For dowel pins, look for SUJ2 at 58 HRC or above — soft imitations wear quickly and locating accuracy degrades noticeably.
3. **Trade spring compression ratio for service life.** The longer the expected life, the lower the compression ratio to use — the 40% tier corresponds to about 1,000,000 cycles, the 45% tier to 500,000 cycles and the 50% tier to 300,000 cycles. Long-term over-compression is the main cause of early spring failure; when the stroke approaches the limit, move up one size. Replace springs of the same set as a set and keep spares.
4. **C7 is a transfer-use grade — do not push it into precision positioning.** Economy rolled ball screws such as the C-TBSB12 suit general feed, lifting and coarse positioning; where micrometre-level positioning is required, upgrade to a precision-grade (C5 / C3) series. Set the target positioning accuracy first, then select the accuracy and backlash grade.
5. **Replace wear-prone standard parts as sets.** Dowel pins, guide post sets, die springs and punches all directly determine die and mould accuracy; mixing old and new causes positioning drift and dimensional instability. Check and replace them as sets on the die maintenance cycle, and list die standard parts — bolts, dowel pins, springs, guide post sets — on one single list to be supplied together, reducing multi-source purchasing.
6. **Bring the mounting datum up to standard first.** The accuracy and life of a miniature linear guide are decided by its mounting surface — bring mounting surface flatness and two-shaft parallelism up to standard before fitting the guide, fix slider and rail against the datum surface, and leave a relief groove or corner radius at the flange to prevent interference.

## FAQ

**Q: What is the difference between CB hex socket head bolts and ordinary hex socket head bolts?**
A: The CB type is MISUMI's fully threaded standard series: M3–M20 reaches grade 12.9 strength, in alloy steel with a special black finish (which makes it easy to distinguish from ordinary bolts and also provides rust protection). The specification system is complete, with 284 selectable variants, suiting standardised purchasing and boxed issue. Ordinary bolts vary in strength grade and surface treatment, and mixing them into critical joints is not recommended.

**Q: What does MS6-20 mean? How do I choose the dowel pin tolerance?**
A: MS is the straight dowel pin type, 6 is a diameter of 6 mm and 20 is a length of 20 mm. Choose the tolerance by the fit with the locating hole: select +0.005 for a precision fit, and +0.010 where a slightly looser fit is needed for easier assembly and removal. For positions with frequent insertion during assembly, select the tapered end; for locating datum surfaces, prefer the R (round) end. When ordering, simply state diameter, length and tolerance together.

**Q: How do I choose between a miniature linear guide and a linear bushing?**
A: Select a linear guide where high positioning accuracy and moment resistance are needed; where the stroke is long, cost is a concern and accuracy requirements are general, a linear bushing can be selected. The two can also be mixed by position, allocated by stroke, load and accuracy requirements. If sticking occurs in micro-motion strokes, it is often related to excessive preload or mounting parallelism out of tolerance — recheck the mounting first, then set the preload grade.
